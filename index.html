ABOUT KERNEL PRIME '26

Where Engineering Meets Futuristic Innovation

Kernel Prime '26 is the flagship national technology conference and 24-hour hackathon hosted by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering & Department of Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) at S.A. Engineering College, Chennai.

Designed as a crucible for semiconductor innovation, embedded electronics, and artificial intelligence, Kernel Prime '26 challenges student teams to prototype real-world solutions under 24 hours with state-of-the-art lab tools, mentorship, and industrial evaluation.
