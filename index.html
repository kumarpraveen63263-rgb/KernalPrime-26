ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Important guidelines regarding team eligibility and participation.

EXTERNAL PARTICIPANTS ONLY

Who Can Participate?

Kernel Prime '26 is exclusively organized to encourage collaboration among students from institutions across India.

Participation is open only to students from external colleges and universities.

Students of S.A. Engineering College are not eligible to participate in this edition.
